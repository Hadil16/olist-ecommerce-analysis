# Executive summary: Olist marketplace performance, Jan 2017 - Aug 2018

**Prepared for:** Marketplace management  **Scope:** 99,092 orders, 96,211 delivered, 13.2M BRL revenue

## Bottom line

The marketplace grew fast through 2017 and then stalled at roughly 0.7 to 1.0M BRL a month. Two problems are holding back the next stage of growth: **late deliveries** that damage customer satisfaction, and a **repeat-purchase rate of only 3%**.

## What we found

1. **Growth was driven by more orders, not bigger baskets.** Jan-Aug revenue grew 141% year over year, but average order value stayed at about 137 BRL. Since Dec 2017 monthly revenue has plateaued.
2. **Late deliveries hurt reviews.** 8.1% of orders arrive after the promised date. These orders average 2.57 stars (vs 4.29 on time) and 46% of them receive a 1-star review. The gap holds in every large state.
3. **Customers rarely return.** Only 3.0% bought twice or more. They account for 5.5% of revenue.
4. **Sales are concentrated.** Sao Paulo is 38% of revenue and three states are 63%. 18 of 74 categories generate 80% of revenue.
5. **Delivery speed depends on region.** The average delivery takes 8.7 days in Sao Paulo and 23.8 days in Para.

## What we recommend

| Priority | Action | How we will know it works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor the late-delivery rate weekly and investigate any month above 10% (seller and carrier level) | Late rate below 5%, average review score up |
| 2 | A/B test a post-purchase email or voucher for first-time buyers | 90-day repeat rate vs the 3% baseline |
| 3 | Improve logistics in slow regions before marketing there | Delivery days and late rate by state |
| 4 | Focus assortment and promotion on the top 18 categories | Revenue share and margin of those categories |

## Caveats

Late delivery and low reviews are associated, which does not prove that one causes the other. The data has no costs, so revenue is not profit. "Late" is measured against Olist's own delivery estimate. The repeat rate covers 20 months of one marketplace.
